HTML(Hyper Text Markup Language):
    *It is a blueprint of website.
    *Hyper text- text which will take us to other pages.
    *Markup-set of symbols inserted in adocument to control its structure.
    *HTML is full of tags and enclosed with /.Eg.<h1></h1>
Tags:
    *Header tag<h1>.Depends on the font the number varies.
    *paragraph tag<p>
    *anchor tag<a href = link>
        which will take us to another page.
    *image tag<img src="image.ext">
        which will make to open a image on our website.
        
    *break tag<br>
        whic is used to skip the line and pass on to next.
    *center tag<center>
        which is used to allign the contents in the center .
    *iframe tag<iframe src= link>
        which is used to connect another website into our website.
    *hr tag<hr> 
        which is used to create a horizontal line.
NOTE:
    *comments can be given by<!-- ..... -->
    attributes:
        *'width=' is used to determine the size of the image.
        *'alt=' is used as an alternative when the expected content is not given.
Lists in HTML:
    In html there are two types of lists'
        *Ordered list.<ol>
            In ordered list it will allign as 
                1...
                2...
        *Unordered list.<ul>
            In unordered list it will allign as 
                .given texthn
